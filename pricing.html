Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical pentest take?

Most Web App or API assessments take between 1–2 weeks. Scope and complexity determine exact timelines, and we always share a clear schedule upfront.

Are your reports accepted by auditors?

Yes. Our reports are structured to meet ISO 27001, SOC2, and GDPR audit requirements. We have worked directly with Big 4 auditors on compliance engagements.

What is a retest and is it included?

After you fix the vulnerabilities we report, we re-verify them to confirm the issues are resolved. All plans include at least one free retest cycle.

How do you protect our sensitive data during testing?

We operate under strict NDAs, and all hackers have undergone background checks. We use isolated testing environments and never retain client data after delivery.
